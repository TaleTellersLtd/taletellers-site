Demos you can open on your phone

These are concept demos: complete, working sites we built to show a Masonic Province and a Scottish plant hire firm what they could have.

Each is labelled as a demo on every page, so nobody takes it for the official site. Open one on your phone and try it.

  1. Provincial Grand Lodge of Linlithgowshire

    Concept demo

    A whole Province on one site: every lodge’s meetings in a single diary, the Province’s notices at the top, and a plain way in for anyone thinking of joining.

    What we built

    • “This week in the Province”: every lodge’s meetings and special events in one calendar
    • Notices such as the Travelling Trowel and the 200 Club, first thing on the home page
    • Pages for the lodges, news, the archive and contact, plus “Thinking of joining?”

    View the concept demo: Provincial Grand Lodge of Linlithgowshire

  2. AB2000

    Concept demo

    A Scottish plant hire firm’s site, built around its hire desk. The phone number stays in the header, and any machine in the fleet list can go straight into a hire enquiry.

    What we built

    • “The fleet at a glance”: excavators, dozers, dumpers, rollers, attachments and sweepers, each with an “Add” button that puts it in your enquiry
    • A quick hire enquiry: what you need, operated or self-drive, where and when
    • Pages for plant, sweeper, transport and vacuum excavator hire, plus news, case studies, vacancies and used plant for sale

Four parts, one platform

Not a brochure you have to phone someone to change. A working set-up your members and customers can carry in their pocket.

  • The website

    Built mobile-first from your own words and photos, with a privacy page included. Every site goes through automated checks for accessibility, broken links and speed before we hand it over.

  • The app, with notifications

    People add the site to their phone’s home screen. Then a lodge can remind its members of the next meeting with one tap. The app is included in the monthly price.

  • The admin panel, with history

    Sign in and change your text, notices and photos yourself. Every change is kept in a history, so anything can be put back.

  • Hosting, on your own domain

    We host the site on Cloudflare Pages: fast, secure and on a global network. The address people type stays the one you own.
