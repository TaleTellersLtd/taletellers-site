Three sites,
built to be used.

The portfolio is the pitch. These are concept demos: complete, working sites we built to show a Province, a lodge and a Glasgow printer what they could have.

None of the three is a client yet, so each is labelled as a demo. Open one on your phone and try it.

  1. 01

    Provincial Grand Lodge of Linlithgowshire

    Concept demo

    A whole Province on one site: every lodge’s meetings in a single diary, the Province’s notices at the top, and a plain way in for anyone thinking of joining.

    What we built

    • “This week in the Province”: every lodge’s meetings and special events in one calendar
    • Notices such as the Travelling Trowel and the 200 Club, first thing on the home page
    • Pages for the lodges, news, the archive and contact, plus “Thinking of joining?”

    View the concept demo: Provincial Grand Lodge of Linlithgowshire

  2. 02

    Lodge St John Mid Calder No. 272

    Concept demo

    A lodge site that answers a member’s first question: when is the next meeting? The date leads the page, one tap from a calendar or from the hall in Maps.

    What we built

    • The next meeting first, with a days-to-go count and the meeting after it
    • “Add to my calendar”, “Open in Maps” and “Get meeting reminders” buttons
    • Pages for office-bearers, Past Masters, the gallery, the 200th anniversary and the lodge’s history

    View the concept demo: Lodge St John Mid Calder No. 272

  3. 03

    William Anderson & Sons Ltd

    Concept demo Preview only: needs the client’s OK

    What we built

    • A home page made from the company’s own photographs of its print
    • “Get a quote” and tap-to-call, fixed to the bottom of the screen on a phone
    • Services, case studies, about and contact pages in the company’s own words

What every site comes with

Four things, on every job, whoever it is for. The screens below are real: a lodge site on a phone, and our admin panel as a club secretary would see it.

An app on the home screen

Your site installs on a phone like an app, and it can send notifications. A lodge can remind its members of the next meeting the day before.

An admin panel you can use

Change your own text, notices and photos, from a phone if you like. Every change is kept in a history, so anything can be undone.

Hosting on Cloudflare Pages

Fast and secure, served from a global network. Nothing for you to install or patch.

Your own domain name

Your address stays yours. The site lives on your domain, and you keep it.

And on every build

Designed for phones first. A privacy page included. Automated checks for accessibility, broken links and speed before we hand it over.
