Chapter II

A small studio with big worlds in mind

TaleTellers is a small independent games studio in the UK, founded by a University of Edinburgh student.

It started with mods for the block-building games we love - and those mods have now passed 300,000 downloads, with around 337,000 across Hyguns, its plugin and our Minecraft mods.

Next, we're building more for Hytale, continuing to make Minecraft mods, and planning our first Roblox games. Every world is a place for players to tell their own stories - that's where the name comes from.

  • Hytale Now

    Hyguns and the Hyguns Plugin, with a server and modding tools planned.

  • Minecraft Ongoing

    All Stone To Tools, All Wools To String and more mods.

  • Roblox Planned

    Our first original Roblox games.
